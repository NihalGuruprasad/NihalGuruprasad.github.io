About

This site is for the projects made by Nihal Guruprasad, both that are currently in development, and as an archive for those that have already been made and the potential code for them also published.

Project #1: The Radio Telescope

This project is about building a radio telescope to monitor the hydrogen spectral line and to use AI to use the observations made from the telescope to make an accurate star map of a specific region of the sky and to possibly predict formations of celestial bodies. Not only will it be able to detect and display the spectral emission line of hydrogen across any portion of the night sky, with a few frequency changes, it should be able to monitor the spectral emission lines of hydroxyl radicals too, making observation of star formation possible and possibly predictable. Through this project, I wish to learn about the methods of star formation and possible ways of cheaply predicting it in the future as well.

Project #2: A Gesturing System for Professionals

This project will be about using the minimum amount of input from the real-life movements of a user to impact the tasks done by their computers, basically observing users' actions and executing respective tasks. This will most probably require the use of a webcam for observation of hand gesturing. This will be an open-source application available to download for Windows users, making it possible to automate tasks that otherwise require undivided and unnecessary attention, speeding up our workflow and making our tasks, much more efficient.

This site is meant as a place to kindle curiosity about these projects, answer other people's queries about them and act as a civilized forum for scientific engagement.
